About Movie Madness

Ever recognized an actor but couldn't remember where you saw them? Search for a film, open its cast, and follow an actor to their other movies. When a title catches your eye, save it for movie night.

Your watchlist stays in this browser. Open it to revisit a title or remove one you've already seen.

I built Movie Madness as my final JavaScript course assignment. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
